A Guide to Writing Your Vows
A little help finding your own words
Your wedding, your words

Let's write vows that sound like you.

This guided workbook will help you collect memories, identify what matters, and find a structure for writing your own vows. It will not write the vows for you.

Step 1

What kind of vows feel like you?

Choose one or more directions. You can combine styles, and you can change your choices later.

You do not need to know exactly what you want to say yet. These choices simply help us decide which questions and writing exercises to include.
Step 2

Tell us about the two of you.

Answer in whatever way feels natural. Bullet points, fragments, and messy notes are welcome. These answers are your raw material, not your vows.

Your answers will appear in your final writing guide as reminders and starting points. You can leave any question blank.
Step 3

Your personal vow-writing guide

This is a path for writing your vows, not a finished set of vows. Your answers are included to help you remember what you want to say.

Remember: Your answers are not your vows. Use them to find the stories, feelings, and promises you want to express. Write in your own voice. Your first draft does not need to be perfect.
